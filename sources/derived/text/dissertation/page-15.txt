unspoken borders between public and 
private, emphasizing the core belief that

However, today both these historical 
areas begin to feel the pressure of 
homogenisation. In Zamalek, residential 
buildings are slowly converting into 
commercial, while façade renovations 
replace original detailing with generic 
finishes. West El Balad also shows a loss 
of architectural identity through 
minimal storefronts and renovations 
done for business and capital. These 
changes slowly kill the history on the 
street and erase the urban memory.
