 
 
Most buildings within the New Capital are identical, more or less. The glass façades, neutral 
material schemes, and generic office and residential blocks. These parts look similar to 
global districts found in larger cities like London, Dubai, and New York, adding to this 
global urban design language. However, this adopted style isn’t made for the climate 
condition of Cairo, relying too much on mechanical cooling, instead of adopting things like 
shading, courtyards, and thick walls that are usually done in Egyptian architecture.
