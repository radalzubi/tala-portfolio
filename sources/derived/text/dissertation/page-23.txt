community driven concept is so much more impactful than any other resistance. It shows a 
greater resistance to homogenisation that comes from inside the community which only 
deepens the value of lived and experienced culture even more. 
 
07 Discussion 
This dissertation was aimed to analyze how architectural homogenisation is changing 
Cairo’s built environment and what that means for cultural identity. Through literature 
reviews, urban analysis, analytical drawings, and architect interviews, homogenisation can 
be seen as a social and spatial concept focusing on profit, developer led decisions, and 
foreign aesthetics of architectural modernity. 
The Urban Analysis shows homogenisation scattered in all areas around Cairo. The New 
Administrative Capital is the most accurate example of generic homogenisation, a large 
scale of repetitive isolated building prioritizing image, convenience, and what will be seen 
as progress over lived experience. It's the spatial logic that truly exposes how that the city 
was designed mainly for show rather than  actual everyday life. This goes along with the 
theory of Koolhaas’ ‘Generic City’, where architecture becomes constantly changing and 
disconnected from community, history, and climate.
