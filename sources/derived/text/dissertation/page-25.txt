08 Conclusion 
While Cairo’s horizons continue to homogenise like those big cities around, the outcome 
may not be so tragic. Today’s skylines may become tomorrow’s memory, therefore creating 
entirely new attachments and cultural meanings. Homogenisation does change Cairo and 
sometimes in a repetitive manner, however through this research of the local practices and 
community engagement possible alternate futures for Cairo’s architectural identity arose. 
Therefore, homogenisation is not a final outcome, but an ongoing back and forth 
discussion. Merely a negotiation between modernisation and memory.  Cairo has the 
opportunity to change repetition into relevance and homogenisation into shared identity.  
Homogenisation is always on the horizon, yet its impact lies on how it is transformed.
