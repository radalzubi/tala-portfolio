Climate responsive architecture can still 
be seen through shaded sidewalks, 
balconies, and  unspoken borders 
between public and private. These 
methods show how historic architecture 
focused on both the environment and 
social life. However modern renovations 
often ignore these strategies and favour 
sealed interiors and modern materials. As 
highlighted by the architects, this change 
reflects the market driven priorities that 
disregard making the architecture deep 
and felt in favour of quicker construction 
and profitability.
