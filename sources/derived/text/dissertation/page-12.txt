 
 
05 Urban Analysis - Case Study 
This urban analysis looks at how architectural homogenisation shows up in different urban 
conditions throughout Cairo. The four main studies previously mentioned, the New 
Administrative Capital, Zamalek, West El Balad, and Ashwa’iyat. They are a contrasting 
range of areas. Using analytical urban drawings, site observation, and photographic 
documentation, this explores spatial, material, social, and climate responsiveness. This 
shows modern developments’ relationship with history and informal urban practices. 
5.1 The New Administrative Capital  
The New Administrative Capital represents the most accurate representation of state led 
and developer driven growth in Cairo. Its goal was to be a symbol of progress, growth and 
modernisation, publicized by its large scale, wide boulevards, and government areas. 
Analytical drawings demonstrate a spatial logic behind it through the isolated buildings and 
how it prioritizes vehicle movement over pedestrians, visual symmetry over social 
environments, and spatial organization over human engagement.
