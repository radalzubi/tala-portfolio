English is also a limitation as the depth and meaning of some of the words may not be as 
dramatic, intense or accurate when translated to another language. Another limitation 
would be the emailed interview with Leithy, a face to face conversation wasn’t conducted 
but a simple paragraph answer to each question, no discussion to understand the thought 
process. While the selected case studies provide depth, they do not represent all urban 
conditions within Greater Cairo. Time issues also limited the extent of on site observations, 
meaning more could have been made. Nevertheless, analytical drawings, interviews, and 
literature strengthens the reliability of the findings by allowing multiple different methods 
of evidence to advise the analysis. 
4.5 Ethics 
This research was conducted in line with the University of Reading’s ethical guidelines. 
Semi structured interviews were carried out with practicing architects who gave their 
informed consent, ensuring that the participants were made aware of the purpose of the 
study and their right to withdraw at any time. Interview data has an optional choice to be 
made anonymous however the architects interviewed claimed to be happy with sharing 
their name. Audio recordings and written materials were securely stored and accessible 
only to the researcher.  
ChatGPT was used as an assistance tool checking translation and content of the recorded 
interviews into transcripts. As well as assisting the selection of relevant architectural 
theories for the literature review from a premade selection list. All theoretical material and 
references were independently sourced.  
Nothing else was involved in the study. The observational and analytical drawings made 
were solely focused on public environments and architecture, avoiding including any 
individuals in the drawings. The research aimed to represent the places and communities 
accurately while ensuring that it is presented in an appropriate manner.
